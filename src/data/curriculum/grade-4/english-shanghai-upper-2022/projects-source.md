# Project 1 Places near my home（P81）

## Think（想一想）

1. Where is your home?
I live in a flat / a house. It’s in the city / in the country.
2. What is around your home? Is there a park, a school, a supermarket or a bakery near your home?
3. What can you see and do in these places?
I can do sport in the park.
I can go shopping in the supermarket.
4. What’s your favourite place near your home? Why?

## Make（制作任务）

Things you need: paper, pencil, crayons
Step 1 Draw your home.
Step 2 Draw roads and places on your map.
Step 3 Write the names of the places. (park, school, supermarket, bakery…)

## Introduce（示范介绍）

This is a map of places near my home. I live in a flat. There is a park, a school and a supermarket near my home. I like the park best. I often do sport there. I can see birds and trees.

## Share（课堂分享）

Show your map to your classmates. Ask and answer questions.

配套词汇：park, supermarket, school, bakery, library

# Project 2 A fancy party（P83）

## Think（想一想）

Plan a fancy party for your class. Discuss these questions:
1. When is the party?
2. Where is the party?
3. What food and drinks do we need?
4. What games can we play?
5. What can we wear at the party?

## Make（制作任务）

Things you need: paper, colour pencils
Step 1 Make a party poster.
Step 2 Draw food, drinks and games on your poster.
Step 3 Write down party information (time, place).

## Introduce（示范介绍）

This is our party poster. Our fancy party is on Friday afternoon. It’s in our classroom. We have juice and cakes. We can play “Guess who”. We can wear our nice clothes.

## Share（课堂分享）

Show your poster to the class. Vote for your favourite party plan.

配套词汇：cake, juice, cookie, game, hat, poster
