## Unit 1 Where do people live? Page1
### Get ready Words
city /ˈsɪti/ 城市
building /ˈbɪldɪŋ/ 建筑物，楼房
flat /flæt/ 公寓
street /striːt/ 街道
country /ˈkʌntri/ 乡村；国家
farm /fɑːm/ 农场
house /haʊs/ 房子
### Sound: a
### Reading: Where’s your home?
—Where do you live?
—I live in a flat in the city.
There are many tall buildings and busy streets.
—Where do you live?
—I live in a house in the country.
There is a farm near my home.
I love my home.

**中文翻译**
—你住在哪里？
—我住在城市里的一套公寓。
这里有很多高楼和繁忙的街道。
—你住在哪里？
—我住在乡村的一栋房子里。
我家附近有一个农场。
我爱我的家。

### Grammar
Where do ... live?
... live ...

### 练习题
1. I ____ (live/lives) in a flat.
2. —Where ____ (do/does) you live?
—I live in the country.
✅答案：1.live 2.do

## Unit 2 Where do animals live? Page11
### Get ready Words
eagle /ˈiːɡl/ 鹰
owl /aʊl/ 猫头鹰
honeybee /ˈhʌnibiː/ 蜜蜂
### Sound: i
### Reading: Animals’ homes
Look at the eagle. It lives in the mountains.
Look at the owl. It lives in the tree.
Look at the honeybee. It lives in a hive.
Where are the rabbits? They are in the hole.
Where’s the bird? It’s in the nest.

**中文翻译**
看这只鹰。它住在山里。
看这只猫头鹰。它住在树上。
看这只蜜蜂。它住在蜂巢里。
兔子在哪里？它们在洞穴里。
小鸟在哪里？它在鸟巢里。

### Grammar
Where’s / Where are ...?
It’s / They’re ...

### 练习题
1. The owl ____ (live/lives) in the tree.
2. —Where are the honeybees?
—____ in the hive. (It’s / They’re)
✅答案：1.lives 2.They’re

## Unit 3 How do we use numbers? Page21
### Get ready Words
odd numbers /ɒd ˈnʌmbəz/ 奇数
even numbers /ˈiːvn ˈnʌmbəz/ 偶数
plus /plʌs/ 加
minus /ˈmaɪnəs/ 减
equal /ˈiːkwəl/ 等于
answer /ˈɑːnsə(r)/ 答案
### Sound: e
### Reading: Numbers! Numbers!
We use numbers every day.
1,3,5 are odd numbers.
2,4,6 are even numbers.
One plus two equals three.
Five minus two equals three.
How many pens do we have?
We have ten pens. We don’t have any pencils.

**中文翻译**
我们每天都使用数字。
1、3、5是奇数。
2、4、6是偶数。
一加二等于三。
五减二等于三。
我们有多少支钢笔？
我们有十支钢笔。我们没有铅笔。

### Grammar
How many ... do we have?
We have ...
... don’t have ...

### 练习题
1. Three plus four ____ seven. (equal / equals)
2. I ____ have any rulers. (don’t / doesn’t)
✅答案：1.equals 2.don’t

## Unit 4 What do we buy? Page31
### Get ready Words
supermarket /ˈsuːpəmɑːkɪt/ 超市
a bag of rice /ə bæɡ ɒv raɪs/ 一袋大米
a box of eggs /ə bɒks ɒv eɡz/ 一盒鸡蛋
a bottle of juice /ə ˈbɒtl ɒv dʒuːs/ 一瓶果汁
### Sound: o
### Reading: Let’s go shopping
Let’s go to the supermarket.
—What would you like?
—I’d like a bag of rice.
—I’d like a box of eggs.
—I’d like a bottle of juice.
—How much is the juice?
—It’s ten yuan.
—How much are the eggs?
—They’re twelve yuan.

**中文翻译**
我们去超市吧。
—你想要点什么？
—我想要一袋大米。
—我想要一盒鸡蛋。
—我想要一瓶果汁。
—果汁多少钱？
—十元。
—鸡蛋多少钱？
—十二元。

### Grammar
What would you like? I’d like ...
How much is/are ...? It’s / They’re ... yuan.

### 练习题
1. I’d like ____ bottle of juice. (a / an)
2. —How much ____ the eggs? —They’re 15 yuan. (is / are)
✅答案：1.a 2.are

## Unit 5 How are the seasons different? Page41
### Get ready Words
spring /sprɪŋ/ 春天
warm /wɔːm/ 温暖的
summer /ˈsʌmə(r)/ 夏天
hot /hɒt/ 炎热的
autumn /ˈɔːtəm/ 秋天
cool /kuːl/ 凉爽的
winter /ˈwɪntə(r)/ 冬天
cold /kəʊld/ 寒冷的
### Sound: u
### Reading: The four seasons
There are four seasons in a year.
In spring, it gets warm. Plants grow.
In summer, it is hot.
In autumn, it gets cool. Leaves fall.
In winter, it is cold. It snows in some places.
It doesn’t snow here.

**中文翻译**
一年有四个季节。
春天，天气变暖。植物生长。
夏天，天气炎热。
秋天，天气转凉。树叶飘落。
冬天，天气寒冷。有些地方会下雪。
这里不下雪。

### Grammar
It gets / snows ...
It doesn’t get / snow ...

### 练习题
1. In spring, it ____ warm. (get / gets)
2. It ____ snow in Guangdong. (doesn’t / don’t)
✅答案：1.gets 2.doesn’t

## Unit 6 What’s amazing about plants? Page51
### Get ready Words
flower /ˈflaʊə(r)/ 花
fruit /fruːt/ 果实，水果
leaf /liːf/ 叶子（复数 leaves）

### Sounds: a, e
### Reading: My sunflower
Look at my sunflower.
It has roots. They hold the plant in the soil.
It has a stem. It has leaves.
It has a big yellow flower.

It doesn’t have fruit.

**中文翻译**
看我的向日葵。
它有根。根把植物固定在土壤里。
它有茎。它有叶子。
它有一朵大大的黄色花朵。
它有种子。
它没有果实。

### Grammar
It has ...
It doesn’t have ...

### 练习题
1. The plant ____ leaves. (have / has)
2. It ____ fruit. (doesn’t have / don’t have)
✅答案：1.has 2.doesn’t have

## Unit 7 How do we keep safe on the road? Page61
### Get ready Words
green light /ɡriːn laɪt/ 绿灯
red light /red laɪt/ 红灯
wait /weɪt/ 等待
cross /krɒs/ 穿过（马路）
### Sounds: i, o
### Reading: Our way to school
We walk to school.
Look at the traffic lights.
Red light, stop.
Green light, cross the road.
Yellow light, wait.
Don’t play on the road. It’s dangerous.

**中文翻译**
我们走路去上学。
看交通信号灯。
红灯，停下。
绿灯，过马路。
黄灯，等待。
不要在马路上玩耍。很危险。

### Grammar
Wait ...
Don’t play ...

### 练习题
1. ____ (Don’t / Not) run on the road.
2. ____ (Wait / Waits) for the green light.
✅答案：1.Don’t 2.Wait

### Get ready Words
go shopping /ɡəʊ ˈʃɒpɪŋ/ 购物

### Sound: u
### Reading: Our grandparents
My grandma often goes shopping.
My grandpa often goes for a walk.
They sometimes do exercise.
They sometimes do housework.
They like to do gardening.
They also use the internet.

**中文翻译**
我的奶奶经常去购物。
我的爷爷经常去散步。
他们有时候锻炼身体。
他们有时候做家务。
他们喜欢打理花园。
他们也会上网。

### Grammar：Review 本册句型总复习

### 练习题
1. My grandpa ____ for a walk. (go / goes)
2. They ____ housework every day. (do / does)
✅答案：1.goes 2.do
