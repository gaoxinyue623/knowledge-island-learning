## Unit 1 Why do people move to new places? 人们为什么搬到新地方？
### Get ready
**Words**
meet the neighbours 认识邻居
make new friends 交新朋友
go ice skating 去滑冰
throw snowballs 扔雪球

**Sounds: ai, ay**
ai /eɪ/ rain
ay /eɪ/ play

### Explore
#### Reading: Postcards to a friend（写给朋友的明信片）
##### Postcard 1
Dear Tom,
My family moved here to Shenyang last week. Everything is new here. Today was a cold winter day. I got up early in the morning. I made a snowman. Then I met my neighbours. We played together. We threw snowballs at each other. In the afternoon, we went ice skating. It was fun.
Love, Rongrong

> 翻译：亲爱的汤姆，我们家上周搬到沈阳。这里一切都是新的。今天是寒冷冬日。我早早起床堆雪人，然后认识了邻居，一起玩耍、打雪仗。下午我们去滑冰，非常好玩。——蓉蓉

##### Postcard 2
Dear Tom,
We moved to Shanghai one month ago. Before that, we lived in a small town. Now we live in a big flat. My parents have new jobs here. I joined a new school and made new friends. I like my new home.
Love, Mike

> 翻译：亲爱的汤姆，我们一个月前搬到上海。之前住在小镇。现在住在大公寓。爸爸妈妈在这里有新工作。我进了新学校，交到新朋友。我喜欢新家。——迈克

#### Grammar
Mike moved to ...
I joined ...
> 一般过去时：描述过去发生的动作。动词变过去式：move→moved；join→joined

### Communicate
- Listening: Marta’s life in China（玛尔塔在中国的生活）
- Speaking: Where you want to move（你想搬到哪里）
- Writing: Moving to a new place（搬到新地方）

### Extend
#### Reading: Moving from here ... to there!（从这里搬到那里！）
Every day, people move to new places. Why do people move? Let’s look at three important reasons.
Cities often have more businesses, so there are more jobs for people. In 2001, around 38 percent of people in China lived in cities. In 2022, that number was around 65 percent.
Last year, my parents found new jobs in Chengdu, so we moved here. I’m in a new school, and I like my new friends.
Some people may move to be closer to better services, such as hospitals and schools.
Some people may move for more space. When I was three, my parents sold our flat in London and bought a house outside the city. There’s lots of space to play football here.
People may also move to learn about another country.

> 翻译：每天都有人搬到新地方。人们为什么搬家？有3个主要原因。城市商业更多，因此工作机会更多。2001年，中国大约38%人口住在城市；2022年，这个数字约65%。去年，我父母在成都找到新工作，我们就搬来了。我在新学校，交到好朋友。有些人搬家，是为了离医院、学校等更好的公共服务更近。有些人搬家，想要更大空间。我三岁时，爸妈卖掉伦敦公寓，在城外买房子，这里有很大空间踢足球。也有人搬家，去了解另一个国家。

## Unit 2 Why do we make art? 我们为什么创作艺术？
### Get ready
**Words**
ink painting 水墨画
oil painting 油画
artist 艺术家
paintbrush 画笔

**Sounds: oy, oi**
oy /ɔɪ/ boy
oi /ɔɪ/ oil

### Explore
#### Reading: The power of art（艺术的力量）
Art can be very powerful. Artists try to express themselves and touch people’s hearts.
Some paintings look real and some don’t. But all paintings show the painters’ feelings.
Xu Beihong was a Chinese artist. He did ink painting. He liked to paint proud animals, such as lions and horses. He wanted people to cheer up and be brave.
Vincent van Gogh was a Dutch artist. He did oil painting. He is famous for using bright colours. Look at the sunflowers in this painting. How does it make you feel? Vincent van Gogh painted flowers in bright colours.
Artists show us the world in their eyes. We can always feel the power of a good work of art.

> 翻译：艺术拥有强大力量。艺术家努力表达自我，触动人心。有些画写实，有些不是，但所有画作都表达画家的情感。徐悲鸿是中国艺术家，画水墨画。他喜欢画雄狮、骏马这类昂扬的动物，希望人们振作、勇敢。文森特·梵高是荷兰艺术家，画油画。他以明亮色彩闻名。看看这幅画里的向日葵。它带给你什么感受？梵高用鲜亮色彩描绘花朵。艺术家向我们展示他们眼中的世界。我们总能感受到优秀艺术作品的力量。

#### Grammar
Vincent van Gogh was ...
He did ...
He painted ...
> 一般过去时：be动词was；实义动词过去式 did / painted

### Communicate
- Listening: Types of art children like（孩子们喜欢的艺术类型）
- Speaking: Why people make art（人们为什么创作艺术）
- Writing: An art form（一种艺术形式）

### Extend
#### Reading: Artists in my family（我家的艺术家）
Anyone can be an artist and make art from anything.
I made something beautiful with just a ball of string. I glued the string in a circle and added a head and a tail. I painted it yellow, blue and green. Now look, it’s a beautiful snail.
My grandma makes art too. She uses old paper and bottles. She reuses old things. It teaches people to protect our earth.

> 翻译：任何人都可以成为艺术家，万物皆可创作。我只用一团绳子做出漂亮作品。我把绳子粘成圆圈，加上头和尾巴，涂上黄、蓝、绿色。你看，一只漂亮蜗牛。奶奶也创作艺术。她用旧纸、旧瓶子创作，废物再利用，提醒大家保护地球。

## Unit 3 How should we protect ourselves? 我们应该如何保护自己？
### Get ready
**Words**
warm up 热身
get hurt 受伤
call for help 呼救

**Sound: ou**
ou /aʊ/ house

### Explore
#### Reading: Student breaks arm while skateboarding（学生滑滑板摔断胳膊）
Yesterday afternoon, Wang Hao, a 12-year-old boy from our school, broke his arm at the skate park. He was playing games with his friends. He tripped over a small stone on the path. Wang Hao got hurt and broke his arm. Luckily, he did not get more hurt because he wore a helmet and pads.
Xu Li, a friend of Wang Hao, called an ambulance. The ambulance arrived soon.
Here are some safety tips for skateboarding.
First, do warm-up exercises.
Next, wear a helmet and pads.
Then, watch out for stones on the path.
Finally, call for help when you get hurt.

> 翻译：昨天下午，我校12岁男孩王浩在滑板公园摔断胳膊。他正和朋友玩游戏，被路上一块小石头绊倒。王浩受伤，胳膊骨折。幸运的是，他佩戴头盔和护垫，没有受更重的伤。王浩的朋友徐丽拨打救护车电话，救护车很快赶到。滑板安全小贴士：首先，热身；其次，戴好头盔护具；然后，留意路上石块；最后，受伤时呼救。

#### Grammar
First, ... Next, ... Then, ... Finally, ...
> 顺序连接词，描述事情先后顺序

### Communicate
- Listening: A brave boy in a fire（火灾里勇敢的男孩）
- Speaking: Protecting yourself（保护自己）
- Writing: Dealing with a fire（应对火灾）

### Extend
#### Reading: Health and safety tips for kids: first aid skills（儿童健康安全小贴士：急救技能）
##### Nosebleed
Sit down. Lean forward. Pinch your nose for 5 to 10 minutes.

##### Ankle injury
First, take off your shoe. Next, put a cool towel on your ankle for 15–20 minutes. Then, lift your ankle and rest.

##### Animal bite
Wash the wound. If the wound bleeds, press it to stop bleeding. After bleeding stops, put medicine on the wound. Cover it with clean gauze. After first aid, you should go to see a doctor.

> 翻译：
流鼻血：坐下，身体前倾，捏住鼻子5-10分钟。
脚踝受伤：首先脱掉鞋子；接着凉毛巾敷脚踝15-20分钟；然后抬高脚踝休息。
动物咬伤：清洗伤口。如果流血，按压止血。止血后上药，用干净纱布包扎。急救完成后，必须就医。

## Unit 4 What makes things move? 是什么让物体运动？
### Get ready
**Words**
push 推
pull 拉
heavy 重的
light 轻的
easy 容易的
difficult 困难的

**Sound: gh**
gh /f/ enough

### Explore
#### Reading: Forces and movement（力与运动）
A force can make something move.
A push is a force. When we push, it moves people or things away from us.
A pull is a force too. When we pull, it moves people or things towards us.
A small force causes a small movement and a big force causes a big movement. When we use a small force to throw a ball, it does not go far. When we use a big force, it goes a long way.
Heavy things are more difficult to move. Light things are easy to move.

> 翻译：力可以让物体运动。推是一种力。当我们推，人或物会远离我们。拉也是一种力。当我们拉，人或物会朝向我们。小的力产生小运动，大的力产生大运动。用很小的力扔球，球飞不远；用力大，球飞得远。重物更难移动，轻物容易移动。

#### Grammar
… is slower than …
… the youngest …
> 形容词比较级、最高级：than用于比较；the+最高级

### Communicate
- Listening: Forces you use to move things（移动物体用到的力）
- Speaking: Forces you use in your daily life（生活中的推力拉力）
- Writing: Forces you use in your daily life（写作：生活中的力）

### Extend
#### Reading: Two stubborn little goats（两只倔强的小羊）
Two little goats were on a narrow bridge. One goat wanted to go east. The other wanted to go west. They met in the middle of the bridge. Neither of them wanted to move. They pushed each other. Both fell into the river.

> 翻译：两只小羊在窄桥上。一只向东，一只向西。它们在桥中间相遇，谁都不肯让路。它们互相推，双双掉进河里。

## Unit 5 How do inventions change our lives? 发明如何改变我们的生活？
### Get ready
**Words**
slide 滑梯
seesaw 跷跷板
watch 手表；观看
lever 杠杆
wheel 轮子

**Sound: ow**
ow /əʊ/ snow

### Explore
#### Reading: Simple machines in our daily lives（日常生活中的简单机械）
What are simple machines? They are the most basic inventions. They make our lives easier, and we use them every day.
The lever: A seesaw is a lever. Two children sit on each side of it. They go up and down. A lever can help us lift heavy things.
The wheel: We use wheels every day on cars, trucks and bicycles. Wheels help us move things easily.
People invented a lot of things to make travel easy. Bicycles, cars and trains help people travel fast on land. Boats and ships help people travel on water. Hot air balloons and planes help people travel in the sky.

> 翻译：什么是简单机械？它们是最基础的发明，让我们生活更轻松，我们每天都在用。杠杆：跷跷板就是杠杆。两个孩子坐在两端，一上一下。杠杆可以帮我们抬起重物。轮子：汽车、卡车、自行车都有轮子。轮子方便我们移动物体。人类发明很多东西方便出行。自行车、汽车、火车帮助人们在陆地快速出行；船在水上出行；热气球、飞机在空中出行。

#### Grammar
Peter runs slowly.
Eddie runs more slowly than ...
Mark runs the most slowly ...
> 副词原级、比较级、最高级

### Communicate
- Listening: Building a tree house（搭建树屋）
- Speaking: The most important invention（最重要的发明）
- Writing: An invention you like（你喜欢的一项发明）

### Extend
#### Reading: Lu Ban and the saw（鲁班和锯子）
One day, Lu Ban went to cut trees. He touched a leaf and hurt his finger.
Lu Ban thought, “How could such a small leaf hurt my finger?” He looked at the leaf carefully. There were many teeth on the leaf, and the teeth were sharp.
“Could I make a tool with teeth like these? It could cut things easily,” Lu Ban said to himself.
Soon Lu Ban made a saw. It was the first saw in the world. People are still using it today.
Lu Ban was not the only ancient Chinese inventor. Other ancient Chinese people invented many great things, such as paper.

> 翻译：一天鲁班上山砍树。他碰到一片叶子，划伤手指。鲁班心想：小小的叶子怎么能割破手指？他仔细观察叶子，叶子边缘有很多锋利小齿。鲁班自言自语：能不能做出带这种齿的工具？它可以轻松切割东西。很快鲁班造出锯子。这是世界上第一把锯，人们至今还在使用。鲁班不是中国古代唯一发明家，古人还发明了纸等伟大物品。

## Unit 6 What is a city? 什么是城市？
### Get ready
**Words**
shopping centre 购物中心
art gallery 美术馆
amusement park 游乐园
historic building 历史建筑
modern building 现代建筑

**Sound: ear**
ear /ɪə/ near

### Explore
#### Reading: Beijing: a big-city snapshot（北京：一座大城市掠影）
Beijing is a very old city with a history of more than 3 000 years. It has been the capital of China for hundreds of years. You can enjoy seeing Beijing's historic buildings. You can learn about China's history at the Palace Museum. Many emperors lived there. Outside the city, you can see the Great Wall. People built it thousands of years ago.
Today, Beijing is one of the most modern cities in the world. There are new roads, tall modern buildings and big shopping centres.
Beijing is a mix of old and new. Nobody can be bored in Beijing!

> 翻译：北京是一座古老城市，拥有三千多年历史，数百年来都是中国首都。你可以欣赏北京的历史建筑，在故宫了解中国历史，很多帝王曾居住于此。城外可以看见长城，数千年前人们修建了它。如今北京是世界最现代化城市之一，有崭新道路、高大现代楼宇和大型购物中心。北京融合古老与现代。没有人会在北京感到无聊！

#### Grammar
There is nothing …
No one could …
Nobody is in …
> 否定代词：nothing 没有东西；no one / nobody 没有人

### Communicate
- Listening: Cities that are near water（临水的城市）
- Speaking: What you can do in a city（你可以在城市做什么）
- Writing: The city you live in or like（你居住或喜欢的城市）

### Extend
#### Reading: From town to megacity（从小城镇到超大城市）
Cities are usually near rivers or lakes. Rivers give people food and water. They are also useful for ships.
Guangzhou sits by the Pearl River. The river helps ships carry goods. Paris sits by the Seine River. Mexico City had a lake long ago. The lake kept the city safe.
What can we do in a city? We can visit museums, go to libraries, watch shows and go to amusement parks.

> 翻译：城市大多临近河湖。河流给人们提供食物和水源，也方便船只通行。广州坐落在珠江边，珠江方便船只运送货物。巴黎在塞纳河畔。墨西哥城很久以前有湖泊，湖泊守护这座城市。我们可以在城市做什么？参观博物馆、去图书馆、看演出、逛游乐园。

## Project 1 (Page 73)

### Project 1：Our new home poster 我们的新家海报

**Aim：Make a poster about moving to a new place.**
目标：制作一张关于搬到新地方的海报。

#### Step 1 Think and collect

Work in groups. Think about these questions:

1. Why do people move to new places?
2. What can you see and do in the new place?
3. How do people feel when they move?

Collect photos or draw pictures of houses, neighbourhoods, parks or people.

#### Step 2 Make your poster

1. Put your pictures on the poster.
2. Write short captions for your pictures.
Use words and sentences from Unit 1.
Example:
We moved to a new town.
We meet new neighbours and make new friends.
We can go ice skating here.

#### Step 3 Show and share

Present your poster to the class.
Tell your classmates about your poster.
Listen to other groups’ presentations.

> 中文翻译
> 项目1：我们的新家海报
> 目标：制作一张关于搬迁到新地方的海报。
> 步骤1 思考与收集
> 小组合作，思考问题：

1. 人们为什么搬到新地方？
2. 在新地方你能看到什么、做什么？
3. 搬家的时候人们感受如何？
收集照片或者画房屋、社区、公园、人物的图画。

步骤2 制作海报

1. 把图画贴到海报上。
2. 为图片写简短说明文字，使用Unit1学过的词句。
示例：我们搬到一座新小镇。我们认识新邻居，结交新朋友。在这里我们可以滑冰。

步骤3 展示分享
向全班展示海报，给同学介绍你的海报。聆听其他小组的展示。

## Project 2 (Page 75)

### Project 2：A city brochure 一座城市宣传小册子

**Aim：Make a brochure about a city.**
目标：制作一份城市宣传手册。

#### Step 1 Choose a city

Work in groups. Choose a city you like. It can be a Chinese city or a foreign city.

#### Step 2 Find information

Find information about your city:

- historic buildings
- modern buildings
- shopping centres
- amusement parks
- art galleries

You can read books, search online or ask people. Write down key information.

#### Step 3 Make your brochure

Your brochure has 3 parts:

1. Cover: city name + picture
2. Page 1: old places in the city
3. Page 2: new places and fun things to do
Use words and grammar from Unit 6.

#### Step 4 Present your brochure

Show your brochure to the class.
Tell your classmates what visitors can do in this city.

> 中文翻译
> 项目2：城市宣传手册
> 目标：制作一份城市宣传小册子。
> 步骤1 选择一座城市
> 小组合作，挑选一座你喜欢的城市，可以是中国城市，也可以是国外城市。

步骤2 查找资料
搜集这座城市的信息：历史建筑、现代建筑、购物中心、游乐园、美术馆。
可以看书、上网搜索或者询问他人，记下关键信息。

步骤3 制作手册
手册分为3部分：

1. 封面：城市名称 + 配图
2. 第1页：城市里的古迹
3. 第2页：现代化场所和好玩的活动
使用Unit6学到的单词和语法。

步骤4 展示手册
向全班展示手册，告诉同学们游客在这座城市可以做什么。

## Grammar review table (Page 81) 语法总表

| Grammar point 语法点 | Examples 例句 |
| --- | --- |
| **一般过去时（规则动词）   Past simple (regular verbs)**   描述过去发生的动作 | Mike moved to Shanghai.   I joined a new school.   move → moved   join → joined |
| **一般过去时：was / did / 动词过去式**   Past simple: was, did, past verbs   描述过去的状态、动作 | Vincent van Gogh was an artist.   He did oil painting.   He painted sunflowers. |
| **顺序连接词   Sequence words   First, Next, Then, Finally**   描述事件先后顺序 | First, warm up.   Next, wear your helmet.   Then, watch out for stones.   Finally, call for help. |
| **形容词比较级 & 最高级   Adjective comparative & superlative**   两者对比 / 三者及以上对比 | A is slower than B.   Tom is the youngest in the group.   heavy → heavier → heaviest   light → lighter → lightest |
| **副词比较级 & 最高级   Adverb comparative & superlative**   描述动作的程度对比 | Eddie runs more slowly than Peter.   Mark runs the most slowly of all. |
| **否定代词   Negative pronouns   nothing / no one / nobody**   表示没有事物、没有人 | There is nothing old here.   No one could be bored.   Nobody is in the room. |

> 中文简要说明

1. 一般过去时（规则动词）：描述过去发生的动作，动词词尾+ed
2. was / did 一般过去式：描述过去状态与动作；was是am/is过去式，did是do过去式
3. 顺序连接词 First首先，Next接着，Then然后，Finally最后，用来描述步骤
4. 形容词比较级、最高级：than用于两者比较；the+最高级用于三者及以上
5. 副词比较级、最高级：修饰动作，多音节副词加more/most
6. 否定代词 nothing没有东西；no one / nobody没有人，本身带否定含义，句子不再加not
