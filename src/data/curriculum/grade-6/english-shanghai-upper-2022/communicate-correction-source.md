## Unit 4 Communicate｜主题：Forces we use to move things

### A Listen, number and complete. Then talk.

听力原文
1. This is a suitcase. We use this when we are on trips. We pull it to move it.
2. This is a door. We push or pull this to go into or out of a room.

填空答案：
We use this when we are on trips.
We push or pull this to go into or out of a room.
重点填空：trips、room。

Speak（对话支架）
A: What do we use a suitcase for?
B: We pull it when we are on trips. Pull is a force.
A: What force do we use for a door?
B: We push or pull the door to go in or out of a room.
句型：We push / pull … to … Push / Pull is a force.

Write（书面任务）
Write about push and pull.
We use forces to move things. We ______ the suitcase on trips. We ______ the door to go into a room. Push and pull are forces.

## Unit 5 Communicate｜主题：How will Lele and his dad use simple inventions to build a tree house?

### A Listen, match and complete. Then say.

听力原文
Lele and his dad will build a tree house.
1. First, they will use the ladder. They will climb up the tree with it.
2. Next, they will use the cart. They will carry the boards with it.

匹配：
1 → ladder
2 → cart

Speak（对话支架）
A: What will they use the ladder for?
B: They will use the ladder to climb up the tree.
A: What will they use the cart for?
B: They will use the cart to carry the boards.
句型：They will use … to …

Write（书面任务）
Lele and Dad will build a tree house. They will use the ladder to ______. They will use the cart to ______. Simple inventions help them a lot.

## Unit 6 Communicate｜主题：Cities are usually near rivers or lakes. Why?

### A Listen and match. Then say.

听力原文
1. The Seine River runs through Paris. It is important for ships to travel on.
2. Lake Texcoco is near Mexico City. It kept the city safe long ago.
3. The Pearl River is near Guangzhou. It gave people food and water.

正确匹配：
按听力编号的正确匹配：
1. The Seine River → Paris → It is important for ships to travel on.
2. Lake Texcoco → Mexico City → It kept the city safe.
3. The Pearl River → Guangzhou → It gave people food and water.

Speak（对话支架）
A: Why are many cities near rivers or lakes?
B: Because water gave people food and water.
A: What about Paris?
B: Paris is by the Seine River. Ships can travel on the river.
句型：… is near … because …

Write（书面任务）
Many cities are near rivers or lakes. ______ is near ______. People can get ______ from the water. Water helps the city.
