> 教材单元：Unit1~Unit6，每个单元独立Communicate板块，包含 Listen（听力原文）、Speak（口语对话）、Write（书面输出）。

## Unit1 Why do people move to new places? 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

Marta moved to China five years ago. Her parents came here to study Chinese. Marta likes living in China. She can speak Chinese now. It’s easy for her to talk with Chinese friends. She loves Chinese food too.

Questions:

1. When did Marta move to China?
2. Why did she move here?
3. What can Marta do now?

### Speak（口语交际）

A: Why do people move to new places?
B: People move for new jobs. Some people move for better schools and hospitals.
A: Where do you want to move?
B: I want to move to Sanya because the weather is warm. When I move there, I can go to the beach every weekend.

句型支架：People move for … / I want to move to … because … When I move there, I can …

### Write（写作任务）

Write about your ideal place to live.
I want to move to ______. I want to live there because ______. I can ______ there. I will make new friends.

## Unit2 Art around us 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

1. Lin likes butterfly art from Mexico. She likes the bright colours.
2. Angel loves Chinese murals from Mogao Grottoes. They are beautiful and full of stories.
3. Vivian likes beetle paintings from Russia. They are special.
4. Justin likes truck art from the US. The paintings on the trucks are amazing.

### Speak（口语交际）

A: What kind of art do you like?
B: I like Chinese paper-cutting.
A: Why do you like it?
B: Because it has beautiful patterns.
A: What art do you want to make?
B: I want to make paper-cutting. It’s fun.

句型支架：… likes … because … / What kind of art do you like? Why?

### Write（写作任务）

Write about your favourite art.
My favourite art is ______. I like it because ______. I want to make ______.

## Unit3 Brave people 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

News reporter: There was a fire in a building. Li Jie saw the fire. He called 119 quickly. He told people to leave the building. Many people got out safely. The firemen put out the fire. Li Jie was very brave.

Questions:

1. What happened in the building?
2. What did Li Jie do?
3. Why was Li Jie brave?

### Speak（口语交际）

A: What did Li Jie do?
B: He saw a fire. He called 119 and told people to leave.
A: Was he brave?
B: Yes, he was. He helped many people.
A: Who is your brave person?
B: My mum. She helps people.

句型支架：What did … do? / … was brave because …

### Write（写作任务）

Write about a brave person.
______ is brave. He/She ______. He/She helped ______. I want to be like him/her.

## Unit4 Healthy living 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

Tom has a good healthy life. He gets up early every morning. He does exercise for 30 minutes. He eats fruit and vegetables every day. He never eats too much sweet food. He goes to bed before ten o’clock. He is strong and healthy.

### Speak（口语交际）

A: What should we do to keep healthy?
B: We should do exercise every day. We should eat plenty of fruit.
A: What shouldn’t we do?
B: We shouldn’t eat too much candy. We shouldn’t stay up late.

句型支架：We should … / We shouldn’t … / To keep healthy, we can …

### Write（写作任务）

Write your healthy rules.
To keep healthy, I should ______. I shouldn’t ______. I will have a healthy life.

## Unit5 Animals in danger 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

Many wild animals are in danger. Pandas have no bamboo to eat. Tigers lose their homes. People cut down forests. We must help them. We can plant more trees. We should not buy things made of animal parts.

### Speak（口语交际）

A: What animals are in danger?
B: Pandas and tigers are in danger.
A: Why are they in danger?
B: Because people cut down forests. They lose their homes.
A: What can we do to help them?
B: We can plant trees. We should protect forests.

句型支架：… are in danger because … / We can … to help them.

### Write（写作任务）

Write about animals in danger.
______ are in danger. They lose their homes. We can ______. We should protect them.

## Unit6 E-friends 【Communicate】

### Listen（听力原文）

Kitty wants an e-friend in the US. She wants to know about her e-friend’s family, school life and hobbies. Joe has an e-friend in the UK. They talk about football. Alice’s e-friend is in Australia. They talk about books and films.

### Speak（口语交际）

A: Would you like to have an e-friend in other countries?
B: Yes. I’d like to have an e-friend in Canada.
A: What would you like to know about your e-friend?
B: I’d like to know about his hobbies and favourite subjects.

句型支架：Would you like to …? / I’d like to … / What would you like to know?

### Write（写作任务）

Write an email to your new e-friend.
Dear friend,
My name is ______. I am ______ years old. I am from China. I like ______. Please tell me about yourself.
Yours,
