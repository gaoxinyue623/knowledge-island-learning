Unit 1 You and me (p.2)

Listen and say

Joe is visiting his cousin George in the countryside.
Joe: I weigh 47 kilograms. How much do you weigh, George?
George: I weigh 40 kilograms.
Joe: I’m 150 centimetres tall. How tall are you?
George: I’m 152 centimetres tall.
Joe: You’re taller. Do you do a lot of exercise?
George: Yes. I usually play basketball and table tennis after school.
Joe: I usually play football after school. I’m a football fan.
George: Sometimes I go fishing with my grandpa.
Joe: How exciting! I don’t go fishing often. There’s no river near my home.
George: But you can go to museums. That’s fantastic!
翻译：乔正在乡下看望表哥乔治。
乔：我重47千克。乔治，你多重？
乔治：我40千克。
乔：我身高150厘米。你多高？
乔治：我152厘米。
乔：你更高。你经常锻炼吗？
乔治：是的。放学后我通常打篮球和乒乓球。
乔：放学后我经常踢足球，我是足球迷。
乔治：我有时候和爷爷去钓鱼。
乔：好刺激！我很少钓鱼，我家附近没有河。
乔治：但你可以去博物馆，太棒了！

Little Leo
Little Leo liked drawing very much. He studied painting in a school.
Every day after school, Leo worked hard. His teacher showed him many paintings.
One day, the teacher asked Leo to draw an egg. Leo thought, “It’s easy to draw an egg.” He drew quickly.
The teacher looked at his picture and said, “Draw it again. You should draw it carefully.”
Little Leo felt unhappy and asked, “Why do we draw eggs all the time?”
“Look carefully! It’s an egg again, but it looks different,” said the teacher.
Leo learned to draw carefully. Later he became a great artist.
翻译：小利奥
小利奥非常喜欢画画。他在一所学校学习绘画。
每天放学后，利奥刻苦练习。老师给他看很多画作。
一天，老师让利奥画鸡蛋。利奥心想：画鸡蛋很简单。他很快画完。
老师看了画说：“重新画，你要仔细画。”
小利奥不开心：“为什么我们一直画鸡蛋？”
老师：“仔细看！这依然是鸡蛋，但每一枚样子都不一样。”
利奥学会仔细作画，后来他成了伟大的艺术家。

Listen and say
