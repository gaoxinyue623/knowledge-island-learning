## 1. Unit 2 Extend

**主题：How can we keep clean?**

### Listen and chant

Wash my face up and down. Wash my face round and round. Wash my face side to side. Wash my face to make it nice.

### Think and say（拓展对话）

—When do we wash our hands? —Before meals. —After you play. —After using the bathroom.

### Task：Clean story

Hello, I’m Mingming. I can wash my face. My face is clean. I’m happy. 句型模板：…, your … is/are … You need to … We can keep clean anytime.

### Letters（自然拼读）

ar /ɑː/ part, art, hot, pot Mom cooks soup in a pot. The soup is hot. Don’t touch the pot.

## 2. Unit 3 Extend

**主题：What do you do in school clubs?**

### Look and learn（拓展词汇）

paper-cutting 剪纸；kick a shuttlecock 踢毽子；draw pictures 画画；sing songs 唱歌

### Talk and share

—What club do you like? —I like the Paper-cutting Club. —What can you do in the club? —I can do paper-cutting.

### Game: Club sharing

Hi! I’m Kate. Look. It’s paper. I can do paper-cutting. Hi! I’m Billy. I can kick a shuttlecock.

### Sounds

un /ʌn/ bun, run, sun Sam has a bun. He goes for a run.

## 3. Unit 4 Extend

**主题：How are people and animals different?**

### Look and retell

Leon is a fish. Leon can swim. Leon can’t run. I’m a boy. I can run. I can’t swim.

### Think and discuss

—What animals can you see? —I can see horses. —What do they have? —They have strong legs. —What can they do? —They can run.

—It has wings. Can it fly? —No, it can’t fly.

### Talk and share

What animals do you like? What do they have? What can they do?

### Sounds

ug /ʌɡ/ bug, mug, hug The bug is on the mug.
