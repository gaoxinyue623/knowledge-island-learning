>全书共6个单元，每单元包含：Listen and point / Chant / Story / Communicate / Think / Extend，**英文原文+中文翻译**，适合跟读背诵。

## Unit 1 What is fun about food? 美食有什么乐趣？
### A Listen, then point and say
Look. There’s lots of food.
There’s rice.
There are fish balls.
There’s congee.
There’s a piece of cake.
I see bread.
I see baozi.
I see dim sum.
**翻译**
看，这里有很多食物。
有米饭。
有鱼丸。
有粥。
有一块蛋糕。
我看见面包。
我看见包子。
我看见点心。

### B Listen and chant
I like fish balls. I like rice.
I like fish balls and rice.
I like congee. I like baozi.
I like congee and baozi.
I like cakes. I like bread.
I like cakes and bread.
I like dim sum. I like dim sum best.
**翻译**
我喜欢鱼丸。我喜欢米饭。
我喜欢鱼丸和米饭。
我喜欢粥。我喜欢包子。
我喜欢粥和包子。
我喜欢蛋糕。我喜欢面包。
我喜欢蛋糕和面包。
我喜欢点心。我最喜欢点心。

### C Story: Jenny’s story (A food festival)
I’m hungry.
Me too. Look, let’s try it.
What is it, Amy?
Oh, it’s congee. I like it.
What would you like?
I’d like some dim sum.
It’s yummy.
**翻译**
我饿了。
我也是。看，我们尝一尝。
艾米，这是什么？
哦，是粥。我喜欢。
你想要点什么？
我想要一些点心。
真美味。

### Communicate (Role play)
—What would you like?
—I’d like some congee. It’s yummy.
**翻译**
—你想要什么？
—我想要一些粥，很好吃。

### Think
What would you like at a food festival?
**翻译**
美食节上你想吃什么？

### Extend
At the Dragon Boat Festival, we eat zongzi.
At the Mid-Autumn Festival, we eat mooncakes.
At Thanksgiving, we eat pumpkin pies.
At Christmas, we eat gingerbread.
**翻译**
端午节，我们吃粽子。
中秋节，我们吃月饼。
感恩节，我们吃南瓜派。
圣诞节，我们吃姜饼。

## Unit 2 How can we keep clean? 我们怎样保持干净？
### A Listen, then point and say
This is the way.
I wash my hands. Early in the morning.
This is the way.
I brush my teeth. Early in the morning.
This is the way.
I wash my face. Early in the morning.
**翻译**
就是这样。
我洗手，在清晨。
就是这样。
我刷牙，在清晨。
就是这样。
我洗脸，在清晨。

### Chant
Wash, wash, wash your hands.
After you play, wash your hands.
Before meals, wash your hands.
Rub, rub, rub your hands.
**翻译**
洗，洗，洗你的手。
玩耍之后要洗手。
饭前要洗手。
搓，搓，搓你的手。

### Story (Sam the pet dog)
Leo, guess, who is coming?
A new family member.
Yes. Welcome your new pet.
Take good care of Sam.
Sam, your paws are dirty. Let’s go and wash.
Let me wash your paws.
It’s bedtime. Let’s brush our teeth.
I brush my teeth every day.
Now your teeth are clean.
Sam, we need to keep clean every day.
This is a story about a dog.
**翻译**
里奥，猜猜谁来了？
一位新家庭成员。
是的，欢迎你的新宠物。
好好照顾山姆。
山姆，你的爪子脏了。我们去洗一洗。
我来帮你洗爪子。
到睡觉时间了，我们刷牙吧。
我每天刷牙。
现在你的牙齿干净了。
山姆，我们每天都要保持干净。
这是一个关于小狗的故事。

### Communicate
—What do you do to keep clean every day?
—I wash my face. I brush my teeth.
**翻译**
—你每天做什么保持干净？
—我洗脸，我刷牙。

### Think
How do you wash your hands?
**翻译**
你怎么洗手？

## Unit 3 What do you do in school clubs? 在学校社团你做什么？
### A Listen, then point and say
We do paper-cutting in this club.
We do the lion dance in the Lion Dance Club.
We’re in the Shuttlecock Club. We can kick shuttlecocks.
**翻译**
我们在这个社团剪纸。
我们在舞狮社团舞狮。
我们在踢毽子社团，我们会踢毽子。

### Chant
Do you want to do paper-cutting?
Join us and have a try.
Enjoy the art of paper-cutting.
Do you want to do the lion dance?
Join us and have a try.
Enjoy the lion dance.
Do you want to kick a shuttlecock?
Join us and have a try.
Let’s have fun together.
**翻译**
你想剪纸吗？
加入我们试一试。
感受剪纸艺术。
你想舞狮吗？
加入我们试一试。
感受舞狮的乐趣。
你想踢毽子吗？
加入我们试一试。
我们一起开心玩耍。

### Story: A secret club
Look, Kate. There’s a new club. Let’s go and see, Billy.
Look! The secret club is in this room.
What do you do in the club?
I do paper-cutting.
Is it a paper-cutting club?
No, it isn’t.
Is it a lion dance club?
No, it isn’t.
Hmm, I have an idea.
Do you kick a shuttlecock in the club?
No, I don’t.
Look! Here’s a bin. What’s in it?
Is this a drama club?
Yes, it is. Come in!
I make masks. I put paper-cuts on the clothes.
Do you like drama? You can join in!
Great!
**翻译**
凯特，看，有一个新社团。比利，我们去看看。
看！秘密社团就在这个房间里。
你们在社团里做什么？
我剪纸。
这是剪纸社团吗？
不是。
这是舞狮社团吗？
不是。
嗯，我有个猜想。
你们在社团踢毽子吗？
不，我们不踢。
看！这里有个箱子。里面是什么？
这是戏剧社团吗？
是的，进来吧！
我做面具，我把剪纸贴在衣服上。
你喜欢戏剧吗？你可以加入！
太棒啦！

### Communicate
—What club are you in?
—I’m in the Paper-cutting Club. I do paper-cutting.
**翻译**
—你参加了什么社团？
—我在剪纸社团，我剪纸。

### Think
What school club are you interested in?
**翻译**
你对哪个学校社团感兴趣？

## Unit 4 How are people and animals different? 人和动物有什么不同？
### A Listen, then point and say
Birds have wings. They can fly.
Horses have strong legs. They can run fast.
Fish have tails. They can swim.
I have legs. I can run. I can swim.
I don’t have wings. I cannot fly.
**翻译**
小鸟有翅膀，它们会飞。
马有强壮的腿，它们跑得很快。
鱼有尾巴，它们会游泳。
我有腿，我会跑，我会游泳。
我没有翅膀，我不会飞。

### B Listen and chant
Birds have wings.
They can fly away.
I don’t have wings to fly away with.
Horses have strong legs.
They can run fast.
I also have strong legs to run fast with.
**翻译**
小鸟有翅膀。
它们可以飞向远方。
我没有翅膀，不能飞向远方。
马儿有强壮的腿。
它们可以跑得飞快。
我也有强壮的腿，可以快速奔跑。

### Story: In the jungle
Emily and Charles are in the jungle.
Look at the monkeys. They can climb trees.
Look at the parrots. They can fly.
Look at the fish. They can swim.
Animals are different. They have different abilities.
**翻译**
艾米丽和查尔斯在丛林里。
看猴子，它们会爬树。
看鹦鹉，它们会飞。
看鱼儿，它们会游泳。
动物各不相同，它们有不同的本领。

### Communicate
—Can it fly?
—No, it can’t. It can run.
**翻译**
—它会飞吗？
—不会，它会跑。

### Think
What can your favourite animal do?
**翻译**
你最喜欢的动物会做什么？

## Unit 5 Who is your hero? 谁是你心中的英雄？
### A Listen, then point and say
My hero is Mrs Li. She’s our teacher.
She can help us learn. She’s warm and nice.
Mrs Zhang takes care of sick people.
She is kind and helpful.
Mr Song can fix things.
He is always happy to help.
**翻译**
我的英雄是李老师。她是我们的老师。
她帮助我们学习，她温柔又友善。
张阿姨照顾病人。
她善良，乐于助人。
宋叔叔会修理东西。
他总是乐于帮忙。

### Chant
Give a hand to others.
Let’s be helpful.
Be nice to others.
Let’s be warm.
Show care for others.
**翻译**
伸出援手帮助他人。
做乐于助人的人。
友善对待别人。
做温暖的人。
关心身边的人。

### Story: Peter’s story
Rainbow Team always wins.
Oh no! We don’t have any water.
Don’t worry. We have some water.
Thanks, Peter. Here you are.
I have an idea.
Hooray! Goal!
Do you think Peter is a hero? Why or why not?
**翻译**
彩虹队总是获胜。
哦，不好！我们没有水了。
别担心，我们有水。
谢谢你，彼得。给你。
我有个主意。
万岁！进球！
你认为彼得是英雄吗？为什么是/为什么不是？

### Communicate
—Who is your hero?
—My hero is my mum. She is helpful.
**翻译**
—谁是你的英雄？
—我的英雄是妈妈，她乐于助人。

### Think
What makes a hero?
**翻译**
什么样的人可以成为英雄？

## Unit 6 Why should we be honest? 我们为什么要诚实？
### A Listen, then point and say
This is a big farm.
There are two farmers on the farm.
There’s a little boy on the farm too.
He watches the sheep.
**翻译**
这是一个大农场。
农场里有两位农夫。
农场里还有一个小男孩。
他看管羊群。

### Story: The boy and the wolf（狼来了）
Boy: Wolf! Wolf! I see a wolf. Help! Help!
Farmers come to help. But there is no wolf.
Boy: It’s a lie. Ha ha!
A new day.
Boy: Wolf! Wolf! I see a wolf. Help! Help!
Farmers come again. No wolf.
The farmers are angry.
One day, a real wolf comes.
Boy: Wolf! Wolf! Help! Help!
No one comes.
**翻译**
男孩：狼！狼！我看见狼了！救命！救命！
农夫赶来帮忙，但是没有狼。
男孩：我骗你们的，哈哈！
新的一天。
男孩：狼！狼！我看见狼了！救命！救命！
农夫又来了，还是没有狼。
农夫很生气。
一天，真的狼来了。
男孩：狼！狼！救命！救命！
再也没有人来帮忙了。

### Communicate
—Why should we be honest?
—Because people will trust us.
**翻译**
—我们为什么要诚实？
—因为大家会信任我们。

### Think
What happens when you tell lies?
**翻译**
当你说谎的时候，会发生什么？

### Extend
Honesty is telling the truth.
Honest people can get others’ trust.
**翻译**
诚实就是讲真话。
诚实的人可以获得别人的信任。
