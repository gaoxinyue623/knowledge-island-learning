## 1. Unit10 Great inventions (p.68)

**Key patterns**

- I think … is a great invention. People can …
- What are you going to invent?
- I’m going to invent something myself.

**Sounds** ch /tʃ/ (chair) sh /ʃ/ (shop)

## 2. Unit11 Chinese festivals (p.74)

**Key patterns**

- … comes in … every year.
- It is an important Chinese festival.
- People call it …

**Sounds** ar /ɑː/ (star) al /ɔː/ (wall)

## 3. Unit12 The giant’s garden (p.80)

**Key patterns**

- A giant lives in a big house with a beautiful garden.
- Children come through a hole.
- The giant is kind to the children.

**Sounds** th /θ/ (think) th /ð/ (this)

## 4. Revision 1 (p.20)

**模块复盘 Module1 Changes and differences**
核心句型汇总：物主代词、Why / Because 问答、一般将来时 will / won’t
重点词汇：tidy, yours, mine, hers, theirs, study, future, machine
语音复盘：ar / ue / oo；ir / ur / au / oor；ee / ea / eer / ear
任务：词汇、句型配对；看图问答；语音朗读练习。

## 5. Revision 2 (p.42)

**模块复盘 Module2 Work and play**
核心句型：be going to（计划打算）；将来特殊疑问句 Where will… / How long will…
重点词汇：storybook, magazine, weekend, film, holiday, seafood, island
语音复盘：i / ie；o / oa / oe；oy / oi
任务：对话补全；周末、假期情景口头表达；语音辨音。

## 6. Revision 3 (p.64)

**模块复盘 Module3 Things we do**
核心句型：will 将来时；选择疑问句；should / shouldn’t 提建议
重点词汇：meet, hall, trousers, sweater, headache, fever, medicine
语音复盘：air / are / ear；ou / ow；ay / ai
任务：情景角色扮演；看图选句子；语音单词分类。

## 7. Project 1 (p.22)

**Project 1: Our changes**
项目主题：记录过去与将来的变化
任务：

1. Draw and write: Write about your changes.
2. Interview your friend: What will you be like in the future?

展示：制作海报，介绍自己和朋友未来的样子。

## 8. Project 2 (p.44)

**Project 2: My holiday plan**
项目主题：假期计划
任务：

1. Make a holiday plan. Where will you go? What are you going to do?
2. Share your plan in groups.

产出：手写假期计划表，小组口头汇报。

## 9. Project 3 (p.66)

**Project 3: Our Open Day**
项目主题：校园开放日
任务：

1. Make an Open Day programme.
2. Talk about what parents will do on Open Day.

产出：设计开放日流程表，小组模拟开放日介绍。

## 10. Project 4 (p.86)

**Project 4: A poster about Chinese festivals / Great inventions**
项目主题：制作海报（二选一：中国节日 / 伟大发明）
任务：

1. Choose one festival or invention.
2. Draw pictures and write short sentences.
3. Introduce your poster to classmates.
