#### Key words
tidy /ˈtaɪdi/ 整理；整洁的

umbrella /ʌmˈbrelə/ 雨伞
hers /hɜːz/ 她的
theirs /ðeəz/ 他们的；她们的；它们的
#### Sounds: ar (car), ue (blue), oo (school)
#### Listen and say
Sally: Look at your bedroom. What a mess! Let’s tidy it up.
Peter: Sure, Sally.
Sally: Whose socks are those? Are they yours, Peter?
Peter: No, they aren’t. They’re Paul’s.
Sally: Can you put them on his bed?
Peter: Sure.
Sally: Whose cap is this?
Peter: It’s mine.
Sally: There’s a T-shirt. Is this yours too, Peter?
Peter: No. It’s Paul’s.
Sally: Can you put it on his bed?
Peter: Sure.
Peter: The room is now clean and tidy. Thank you for your help, Sally.
Sally: You’re welcome, Peter.

**中文翻译**
萨莉：看看你的卧室，一团糟！我们来整理一下吧。
彼得：好的，萨莉。
萨莉：那些是谁的袜子？是你的吗，彼得？
彼得：不是，是保罗的。
萨莉：你可以把袜子放到他床上吗？
彼得：好的。
萨莉：这是谁的帽子？
彼得：是我的。
萨莉：这里有一件T恤。这也是你的吗，彼得？
彼得：不是，是保罗的。
萨莉：你把它放到他床上好吗？
彼得：没问题。
彼得：房间现在干净整齐了。谢谢你帮忙，萨莉。
萨莉：不客气，彼得。

#### Key patterns
—Are they yours?
—No, they aren’t.
#### 小练习
1. This umbrella is ____. (mine / my)
2. —Are those crayons theirs? —Yes, ____. (they are / it is)
✅答案：1.mine 2.they are

### Unit12 The giant’s garden (p.80)
#### Key words
wall /wɔːl/ 墙
kind /kaɪnd/ 友善的
through /θruː/ 穿过
#### Listen and say
A giant lives in a big house with a beautiful garden. Children like to play in the garden. They have a lot of fun there.
The giant goes away. When he comes back, he sees the children in his garden. He is very angry.
Giant: This is my garden! You can’t play here.
He builds a high wall around the garden. Spring does not come to the giant’s garden. It is always winter there.
One morning, the giant hears some lovely sounds. He sees some beautiful flowers too. The giant is very happy.
Giant: Hooray! Spring is here!
The giant finds some children in his garden. They are coming through a hole. They bring Spring to his garden!
The giant knocks down the wall around his garden.
Giant: Children, you can play in my garden any time.

**中文翻译**
巨人住在一栋大房子里，房子配有美丽花园。孩子们喜欢在花园玩耍，玩得很开心。
巨人外出，回来的时候看见孩子们在花园里，他非常生气。
巨人：这是我的花园！你们不能在这里玩。
他在花园四周筑起高墙。春天再也不来巨人的花园，那里永远是冬天。
一天早上，巨人听见悦耳的声音，还看见了美丽的花朵。巨人十分开心。
巨人：太好了！春天来了！
巨人看见花园里有几个孩子，他们从墙洞里钻进来，是他们把春天带进花园！
巨人推倒了围墙。
巨人：孩子们，你们随时都可以来我的花园玩耍。

#### 小练习
1. The giant ____ a wall. (build / builds)
2. Children come ____ a hole. (through / on)
✅答案：1.builds 2.through
