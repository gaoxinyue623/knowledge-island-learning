## Unit 1 What do we do at the weekend? Page1
### Get ready Words
go jogging /ɡəʊ ˈdʒɒɡɪŋ/ 慢跑
go fishing /ɡəʊ ˈfɪʃɪŋ/ 钓鱼
do chores /duː tʃɔːz/ 做家务
watch TV /wɒtʃ ˌtiː ˈviː/ 看电视
play with friends /pleɪ wɪð frendz/ 和朋友玩耍
have a picnic /hæv ə ˈpɪknɪk/ 野餐
### Sounds: c, g
### Reading: Weekend plans
People do fun activities at the weekend.
What are these children going to do this weekend?
This weekend, I'm going to go jogging with my family in the park.
We must get up early on Sunday morning and warm up. It's going to be fun!
This weekend, my dad and I are going to go fishing at Blue Lake.
I can't wait to catch more fish than my dad.
I'm going to have a picnic with my family this weekend.
We can fly a kite in the park. Hurry up, Saturday!
Today is a busy day! We're all going to do chores.

**中文翻译**
人们在周末开展有趣的活动。
这些孩子这个周末打算做什么？
这个周末，我打算和家人去公园慢跑。
周日早上我们必须早起热身。一定会很好玩！
这个周末，我和爸爸打算去蓝湖钓鱼。
我迫不及待想钓比爸爸更多的鱼。
这个周末我要和家人去野餐。
我们可以在公园里放风筝。快点到来吧，星期六！
今天会很忙碌！我们都要做家务。

### Grammar
I am / He is / She is / We are going to …（一般将来时：打算、将要做）
### 小练习
1. I ____ (am / is) going to watch TV.
2. They ____ (are / is) going to have a picnic.
✅答案：shtu 2.are

## Unit 2 How do we stay healthy? Page13
### Get ready Words
do exercise /duː ˈeksəsaɪz/ 锻炼身体
eat healthy food /iːt ˈhelθi fuːd/ 吃健康食物
drink a lot of water /drɪŋk ə lɒt ɒv ˈwɔːtə(r)/ 大量喝水
get enough sleep /ɡet ɪˈnʌf sliːp/ 保证充足睡眠
### Sounds: th
### Reading: Healthy or not?
Healthy children stay strong.
What are you going to do to stay healthy?
I am going to do exercise every day.
I am going to eat healthy food. Fruit and vegetables are good for me.
I am going to drink a lot of water.
I am going to get enough sleep.
What is he going to do?
He is going to play computer games all day. That’s not healthy.
What is she going to do?
She is going to eat too much sweet food. That’s not healthy.

**中文翻译**
健康的孩子身体强壮。
你打算做些什么来保持健康？
我打算每天锻炼身体。
我打算吃健康食物。水果和蔬菜对我有益。
我打算多喝白开水。
我打算保证充足睡眠。
他打算做什么？
他打算一整天玩电脑游戏。这不健康。
她打算做什么？
她打算吃太多甜食。这不健康。

### Grammar
What are you going to do? We are / I am going to …
What is he / she going to do? He / She is going to …
### 小练习
1. She ____ (is / are) going to eat healthy food.
2. —What ____ you going to do? —I’m going to exercise. (am / are)
✅答案：shtu 2.are

## Unit3 What do you know about different countries? Page25
### Get ready Words
China /ˈtʃaɪnə/ 中国
France /frɑːns/ 法国
UK /ˌjuː ˈkeɪ/ 英国
north /nɔːθ/ 北方
south /saʊθ/ 南方
east /iːst/ 东方
west /west/ 西方
### Sound: ss
### Reading: Countries around the world
Look at the map of the world.
China is in the east of Asia.
The UK is in the west of Europe.
France is also in Europe. It is to the south of the UK.
Canada is in the north of North America.
Australia is in the south of the world.
Different countries have different beautiful places.

**中文翻译**
看这张世界地图。
中国位于亚洲东部。
英国位于欧洲西部。
法国也在欧洲。它在英国的南边。
加拿大位于北美洲北部。
澳大利亚在南半球。
不同的国家有着不同美丽的地方。

### Grammar
China is in the east of Asia.（方位表达：in the +方位 + of…）
### 小练习
1. France is ____ the south of the UK. (in / to)
2. China is in the ____ of Asia. (east / south)
✅答案：1.to 2.east

## Unit4 What’s interesting about holidays? Page37
### Get ready Words
mountain /ˈmaʊntən/ 高山
cave /keɪv/ 洞穴
waterfall /ˈwɔːtəfɔːl/ 瀑布
hotel /həʊˈtel/ 酒店
### Sounds: sh, ch
### Reading: A holiday plan
The summer holiday is coming.
How will you spend your holiday?
Where will you go?
I will go to Guilin. I will see beautiful mountains and rivers.
I will go to Huangshan. I will visit the mountains and see strange rocks.
I will go to Jiuzhaigou. I will see waterfalls.
I will stay in a nice hotel.
I will take photos. I will have great fun!

**中文翻译**
暑假快要来了。
你会如何度过假期？
你要去哪里？
我会去桂林。我会欣赏秀美的山水。
我会去黄山。我游览群山，观赏奇石。
我会去九寨沟。我去看瀑布。
我会住在一家舒适的酒店里。
我会拍照。我会玩得非常开心！

### Grammar
How will you …? Where will you …? What will you …? I will …（will 一般将来时）
### 小练习
1. I ____ (will / am) visit the mountain.
2. —Where ____ you go? —I will go to Guilin. (will / are)

## Unit5 What are living things? Page49
### Get ready Words
get sunlight /ɡet ˈsʌnlaɪt/ 获取阳光
get food and water /ɡet fuːd ənd ˈwɔːtə(r)/ 获取食物和水
grow /ɡrəʊ/ 生长
change /tʃeɪndʒ/ 变化
move /muːv/ 移动
### Sounds: w, wh
### Reading: Living and non-living things
Living things are plants and animals.
Living things get sunlight, food and water.
They grow. They change. They can move.
A bird is a living thing. It can fly. It grows.
A tree is a living thing. It grows. It gets sunlight.
A rock is not a living thing. It does not grow. It does not move.
A toy car is not a living thing. It cannot grow or change.

**中文翻译**
生物包括植物和动物。
生物获取阳光、食物和水。
它们会生长、变化，能够移动。
小鸟是生物。它会飞，会长大。
大树是生物。它会生长，获取阳光。
石头不是生物。它不会生长，不会移动。
玩具汽车不是生物。它不能生长，也不会发生变化。

### Grammar
and, or, but 连词
### 小练习
1. Plants grow ____ get sunlight. (and / but)
2. A toy cannot grow ____ change. (or / and)
✅答案：1.and 2.or

## Unit6 Why should we take care of the Earth? Page61
### Get ready Words
smoke /sməʊk/ 烟雾
dirty /ˈdɜːti/ 脏的
rubbish /ˈrʌbɪʃ/ 垃圾
recycle /ˌriːˈsaɪkl/ 回收利用
reduce /rɪˈdjuːs/ 减少
### Sounds: ng, nk
### Reading: Natural resources
The Earth gives us many natural resources.
We get water, wood and coal from the Earth.
But we make the Earth dirty. Smoke and rubbish are bad for it.
We should take care of the Earth.
We should reduce waste.
We should recycle paper and plastic.
We shouldn’t throw rubbish everywhere.
We shouldn’t cut down too many trees.

**中文翻译**
地球给我们提供很多自然资源。
我们从地球上获取水、木材和煤炭。
但是我们把地球弄脏了。烟雾和垃圾对地球有害。
我们应当爱护地球。
我们应当减少浪费。
我们应当回收纸张和塑料。
我们不应该到处乱扔垃圾。
我们不应该砍伐太多树木。

### Grammar
We should … We shouldn’t …（should应该 / shouldn’t不应该）
### 小练习
1. We ____ recycle rubbish. (should / shouldn’t)
2. We ____ cut too many trees. (shouldn’t / should)
✅答案：1.should 2.shouldn’t

## Unit7 How do we celebrate festivals? Page73
### Get ready Words
Spring Festival /sprɪŋ ˈfestɪvl/ 春节
Lantern Festival /ˈlæntən ˈfestɪvl/ 元宵节
Dragon Boat Festival /ˈdræɡən bəʊt ˈfestɪvl/ 端午节
Mid-Autumn Festival /ˌmɪd ˈɔːtəm ˈfestɪvl/ 中秋节
### Sentence stress：句子重音
### Reading: Important festivals
We have many important festivals in China.
At the Spring Festival, we have a big family dinner.
We visit relatives.
At the Lantern Festival, we watch lanterns and eat tangyuan.
At the Dragon Boat Festival, we have dragon boat races and eat zongzi.
At the Mid-Autumn Festival, we look at the moon and eat mooncakes.
We will have the Mid-Autumn Festival in one month.

**中文翻译**
在中国，我们有很多重要节日。
春节，我们吃丰盛的年夜饭，走亲访友。
元宵节，我们赏花灯，吃汤圆。
端午节，我们举办龙舟赛，吃粽子。
中秋节，我们赏月，吃月饼。
再过一个月我们就要过中秋节了。

### Grammar
Time words for future: in one hour, next month …（将来时间短语）
### 小练习
1. We ____ (will / are) have the festival next month.
2. We eat mooncakes ____ the Mid-Autumn Festival. (at / in)
✅答案：1.will 2.at

## Unit8 How does music make us feel? Page85
### Get ready Words
fast /fɑːst/ 快的
slow /sləʊ/ 慢的
excited /ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/ 兴奋的
unhappy /ʌnˈhæpi/ 不开心的
### Sounds: Loss of plosion（失去爆破）
### Reading: How music makes us feel
Music can make us feel different ways.
Fast music can make us feel excited.
Loud drums and fast music make people excited.
Slow music can make us feel quiet and relaxed.
Soft piano music is slow. It makes me calm.
Sad music can make us feel unhappy.
Happy music can make us feel glad.
Different music brings different feelings.

**中文翻译**
音乐可以带给我们不同的感受。
节奏快的音乐能让我们感到兴奋。
响亮的鼓点和快节奏音乐使人兴奋。
舒缓的音乐可以让我们安静放松。
轻柔的钢琴曲节奏缓慢，使我平静。
悲伤的音乐能让我们感到难过。
欢快的音乐能让我们感到愉悦。
不同的音乐带来不一样的感受。

### Grammar：Review（本册全部句型总复习）
### 小练习
1. Fast music makes me ____. (excited / slow)
2. Slow music makes me ____. (relaxed / unhappy)
✅答案：1.excited 2.relaxed
