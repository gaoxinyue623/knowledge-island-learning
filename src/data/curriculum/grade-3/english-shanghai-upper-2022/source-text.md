## Unit 1 How do we feel?（Page5）

### Get ready Words

happy 开心的，sad 难过的，angry 生气的，tired 累的

### How are you?

Good morning, boys and girls! How are you? —Not so good. I’m cold. —OK, Wen Jie. Let’s close the window. —I’m sad. I can’t find my pencil. —Look, Jia Li. Here’s a pencil. —What about you two? —I’m happy. —I’m happy too! —Well, let’s start again. How are you? —Fine, thank you. —Phew! Now please look at the blackboard.

中文翻译
早上好，同学们！你们好吗？ ——不太好。我很冷。 ——好的，文杰。我们把窗户关上。 ——我很难过。我找不到我的铅笔。 ——看，佳丽。这有一支铅笔。 ——你们两个呢？ ——我很开心。 ——我也很开心！ ——好了，我们重新开始。你们好吗？ ——很好，谢谢你。 ——呼！现在请看黑板。

### Grammar

How are you? I am …（happy/sad/angry/tired）

## Unit 2 What’s interesting about families?（Page15）

### Get ready Words

grandfather 爷爷/外公，grandmother 奶奶/外婆，father 爸爸，mother 妈妈，sister 姐妹，brother 兄弟，me 我，grandpa 爷爷/外公，grandma 奶奶/外婆

### Families

This is my family. Look! He is my grandfather. She is my grandmother. This is my father. This is my mother. This is my brother and this is my sister. Who is he? He is my father. Who is she? She is my mother.

中文翻译
这是我的家庭。 看！他是我的爷爷。 她是我的奶奶。 这是我的爸爸。这是我的妈妈。 这是我的弟弟，这是我的妹妹。 他是谁？他是我的爸爸。 她是谁？她是我的妈妈。

### Grammar

This is my family. Who is he/she? He/She is …

## Unit 3 What do we look like?（Page25）

### Get ready Words

head 头，hair 头发，eye 眼睛，ear 耳朵，nose 鼻子，mouth 嘴巴，face 脸

### Minmin’s haircut

Look at your hair, Minmin. It’s too long. But I like it. Minmin, I can’t see your eyes. Now sit on this chair, please. Look! Do you like your haircut? Oh! I have short hair. I like my haircut, Mum. You look great! Thanks, Mum.

中文翻译
看看你的头发，敏敏。它太长了。 但是我喜欢它。 敏敏，我看不见你的眼睛。现在请坐在椅子上。 看！你喜欢你的新发型吗？ 哦！我有短头发了。 妈妈，我喜欢我的发型。 你看起来很棒！ 谢谢妈妈。

### Grammar

I have …（I have long hair. I have big eyes.）

## Unit 4 How do we have fun?（Page35）

### Get ready Words

skip 跳绳，run 跑步，fly a kite 放风筝，skateboard 滑滑板，ride a bike 骑自行车

### In the park

We are in the park. I can skip. I can run. I can fly a kite. He can skateboard. She can ride a bike. I can skip. But I can’t skateboard.

中文翻译
我们在公园里。 我会跳绳。 我会跑步。 我会放风筝。 他会滑滑板。 她会骑自行车。 我会跳绳。但是我不会滑滑板。

### Grammar

I / He / She can … I / He / She can’t …

## Unit 5 What do we eat?（Page45）

### Get ready Words

milk 牛奶，juice 果汁，apple 苹果，banana 香蕉，grape 葡萄，tomato 番茄，carrot 胡萝卜，fish 鱼，meat 肉

### A funny food face

Look! A funny food face. I like apples. I like bananas. What do you like? I like milk. I like juice. Do you like fish? Yes, I do. Do you like meat? No, I don’t.

中文翻译

### Grammar

What do you like? I like … Do you like …? Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

I’m Coco. I’m in the park. What is it? It is a rabbit. It can jump. Look! What are they? They are squirrels. They can climb. I like rabbits. I like squirrels.

## Unit 7 What do we know about weather?（Page65）

### Get ready Words

### Grammar

How is the weather? It is …
