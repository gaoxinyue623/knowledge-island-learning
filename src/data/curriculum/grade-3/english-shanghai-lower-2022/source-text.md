## Unit 1 How do we spend our free time?（Page1）
### Get ready Words
read /riːd/ 阅读
sing /sɪŋ/ 唱歌
paint /peɪnt/ 画画
dance /dɑːns/ 跳舞
swim /swɪm/ 游泳
play basketball /pleɪ ˈbɑːskɪtbɔːl/ 打篮球
play ping-pong /pleɪ ˈpɪŋ pɒŋ/ 打乒乓球
play football /pleɪ ˈfʊtbɔːl/ 踢足球

### Sounds: p, b
### Reading: What are your hobbies?
Hello children! Welcome to our centre. Let’s talk about hobbies. What do you like doing in your free time?
I like music. I like singing and dancing. This is me in my dance class. Look at my purple skirt!
I like reading. I like painting too. This is me in my favourite park.
I’m good at sport. I like playing ping-pong and riding my bike. This is me on my bike.
I like sport. I swim a lot. I play basketball too. This is me at the basketball court.

**中文翻译**
孩子们好！欢迎来到活动中心。我们聊聊爱好吧。空闲的时候你喜欢做什么？
我喜欢音乐。我喜欢唱歌跳舞。这是我在舞蹈课上，看我的紫色短裙！
我喜欢阅读，也喜欢画画。这是我在最喜欢的公园里。
我擅长运动。我喜欢打乒乓球和骑自行车。这是骑车的我。
我喜欢运动。我经常游泳，也打篮球。这是我在篮球场。

### Grammar
What do you like doing?
I like …(doing)

### Sounds: s, z
### Reading: Nini’s new school
Welcome to my new school.
Is there a library in your school?
Yes, there is. We read books there.
Are there any computer rooms?
Yes, there are.
Is there a sports ground?
No, there isn’t. But we have a big playground.

**中文翻译**

你们学校有图书馆吗？
是的，有。我们在那里看书。
有电脑室吗？
是的，有。
有运动场吗？
不，没有。但是我们有一个大操场。

### Grammar
